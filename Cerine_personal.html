About me

Hi! I am Cerine, second-year AI student at Beijing Institute of Technology. I’ve always been interested in technology, but I also really enjoy creative things, so AI is a field I find especially interesting because it combines both. Outside of university, I spend a lot of my time doing things I enjoy creatively. Photography is one of my favorite hobbies, and I also like digital art, crafts, and design. I enjoy experimenting with different styles and trying out new things, especially when I can turn an idea I have into something visual or interactive. For Codyssey, I’m the CTO of Team Strawberry Shortcake. I mainly work on the technical side of the project, helping build the website and making sure everything works the way we planned. I also take part in the design and creative decisions because I really enjoy that side of the project too. Being able to work on both the technical and creative parts is probably what I enjoy most about Codyssey.
