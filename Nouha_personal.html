About me

Hi! I’m a second-year AI student at Beijing Institute of Technology. I’m interested in both the creative and technical sides of technology, especially artificial intelligence and its different applications. What I enjoy most about AI is how it can be used not only to solve problems, but also as a tool for creativity, experimentation, and bringing new ideas to life. Outside of studying, I love anything that involves creativity. I enjoy painting, drawing, crafts, design, and generally trying out new ways to make things. I like exploring different styles and ideas, and I often find myself combining my creative interests with technology. For me, the most interesting projects are the ones that are both functional and visually engaging. For our project *Codyssey*, I’m the CEO of Team Strawberry Shortcake. My role involves helping coordinate the team, organizing our ideas, keeping track of our progress, and making sure everything comes together as a whole. At the same time, I’m also one of the developers, so I get to work directly on the website and turn our ideas into something functional. I enjoy being involved in both the creative and technical sides of the project.
